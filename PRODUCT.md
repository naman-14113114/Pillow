# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

UK shoppers arriving with strong side-sleeper and pillow-comparison intent. They need to understand the OrthoAlign shape, choose a suitable height and colour, and move to checkout without ambiguity.

## Product Purpose

Present one focused pillow as a credible, premium and easy-to-understand choice. Success means the product is visually obvious, the selection logic is clear, and the path from discovery to cart feels consistent with the Buudy and Muuhu storefronts.

## Brand Personality

Calm, tactile and assured. Juujo should feel like a considered sleep product rather than a clinical aid or a generic dropshipping template.

## Anti-references

No orange or purple theme, no invented pillow contours, no visible branding printed on the pillow, no undersized typography, no repetitive card grids, no image collages used as a substitute for art direction, and no repeated lifestyle image on one page.

## Design Principles

1. Show the real product before explaining it.
2. Use one decisive lifestyle image per story beat.
3. Make Regular versus High and the four colours understandable at a glance.
4. Keep conversion controls clear without turning the homepage into a second product page.
5. Preserve the established deep-navy, royal-blue, powder-blue and white identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, keyboard access, reduced-motion support, readable type at mobile sizes and descriptive alternative text for product imagery.
