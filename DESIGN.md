# Juujo Visual System

## Direction

An image-led UK sleep storefront with a calm, premium tone. Full-width photography carries the emotional story; clear product diagrams and restrained controls carry the decision-making story.

## Colour

- Deep navy: `#06173b`
- Royal blue: `#0757d9`
- Powder blue: `#dceeff`
- Sky blue: `#85c6ff`
- Mist: `#f7fbff`
- Ink: `#10223f`
- Muted ink: `#60708a`

Orange and purple are excluded from the interface palette.

## Typography

Use the existing Melbourne display family for confident headings and Inter for body copy, controls and navigation. Homepage headings should be visibly larger than compact product controls, with body copy kept at a comfortable 16 to 18 px on desktop and at least 15 px on mobile.

## Layout

- Let the homepage hero sit beneath a transparent navigation layer.
- Prefer full-width bands and asymmetric image-and-copy compositions over floating cards.
- Use one image per narrative section; do not build lifestyle collages.
- Keep fixed image ratios and consistent grid tracks so reviews and controls do not jump.
- Show a hint of the next homepage section in the first viewport.

## Components

- Primary actions use royal blue with white text.
- Secondary actions use visible text links or quiet outlined controls.
- Colour choices use labelled swatches.
- Height choices use a two-option segmented control or a direct Regular/High comparison.
- Cards are reserved for individual reviews, bundle options and framed tools.

## Motion

Use short opacity and transform reveals with ease-out timing. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; do not animate layout dimensions.
